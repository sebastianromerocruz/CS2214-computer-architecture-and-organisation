# Adders

*Stub — taught in Week 2 (cont.)*

Combinational circuits that perform binary addition using [[logic gates]]. Half adder: 1-bit + 1-bit → sum + carry. Full adder: adds carry-in. Ripple-carry adder: chain of full adders for N-bit addition.

**Related:** [[Logic Gates]], [[Two's Complement]], [[Boolean Algebra]]
