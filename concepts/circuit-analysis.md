# Circuit Analysis

Reading a circuit diagram and extracting its [[Boolean Algebra|Boolean expression]] and [[Truth Tables|truth table]]. The reverse — building a circuit from a Boolean expression — is circuit synthesis, which is what [[Verilog]] tools do automatically.

**The skill:** trace signal flow gate by gate, name intermediate wires, compose into one expression.

**First taught:** [[Week 1 - Logic Gates]]
**Related:** [[Logic Gates]], [[Boolean Algebra]], [[Truth Tables]], [[Verilog]]
