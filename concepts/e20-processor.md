# E20 Processor

*Stub — taught in Weeks 4–6*

A 16-bit educational processor, the successor to [[E15 Processor]]. Adds a full datapath, memory, and enough complexity to study [[Pipelining]]. The E20 is the main vehicle for understanding how a real processor is implemented.

**Related:** [[E15 Processor]], [[Pipelining]], [[Registers]], [[Sequential Logic]]
