# E15 Processor

*Stub — taught in Weeks 3–4*

A custom 12-bit educational processor designed for CS2214. Small enough to understand completely: 4-bit data registers (Rg0–Rg3), 4-bit program counter, 16-word instruction memory, 11 instructions.

**Instruction format:** `{opcode[3:0], src[1:0], dst[1:0], imm[3:0]}`
**Purpose:** teaches fetch-decode-execute cycle and ISA design at a scale you can trace by hand.
