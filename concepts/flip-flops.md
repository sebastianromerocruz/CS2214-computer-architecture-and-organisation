# Flip-Flops

*Stub — taught in Week 3*

A 1-bit [[Sequential Logic|sequential]] storage element. The D flip-flop captures input D at the rising clock edge and holds it on output Q until the next edge. The fundamental building block of [[Registers]] and all stateful circuits.

**Related:** [[Sequential Logic]], [[Registers]], [[Verilog]], [[E15 Processor]]
