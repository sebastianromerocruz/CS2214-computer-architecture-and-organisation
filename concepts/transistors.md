# Transistors

Semiconductor devices that act as electrically controlled switches. The voltage on the gate terminal controls whether current flows between source and drain. Wired in specific configurations, transistors implement [[logic gates]]. A modern CPU contains tens of billions.

**Historical note:** Replaced vacuum tubes in the 1950s. The first transistor fit in your palm; modern ones are measured in nanometres.
